How it works. Your flight follows the shortest route between the airports at 840 km/h, plus 30 minutes for taxi and climb. Every 5 minutes at cruise, the guide checks whether the sun is up where the plane is. Winds are ignored, so eastbound flights over the Atlantic arrive a little earlier than shown.

Persistent contrails only form in cold, humid air, which can't be forecast weeks ahead. The guide shows when a contrail would do the most harm, not whether one will form.
